Problem Description

The revenue of a shop on day i is a[i] (i = 1, 2, . . ., n). Given a positive integer K, a K-period is a sequence of K consecutive days of 1, 2, . . ., n. 

Given a threshold D (positive integer), compute the nunmber Q of K-periods in which the total revenue is less than or equal to D.

Input

  • Line 1: contains 3 positive integers n, K and D (1 <= n <= 100000, 1 <= K <= n/2, 1 <= D <= 1000000)
  • Line 2: contains n integers a[1], a[2], . . ., a[n] (1 <= a[i] <= 10000)

Output

  • Write the value Q

Example

Input

7 3 10

6 3 4 2 3 3 5 

Output

3

Explanation: there are three 3-periods satisfying the constraint:

3 4 2

4 2 3

2 3 3
