Problem Description

The revenue of a shop on day i is a[i] (i = 1, 2, . . ., n). Given a threshold D (positive integer), compute the nunmber Q of consecutive days in which the total revenue is less or equal to D.

Input

  • Line 1: contains 2 positive integers n and D (1 <= n <= 1000, 1 <= D <= 1000)
  • Line 2: contains n integers a[1], a[2], . . ., a[n] (1 <= a[i] <= 100)

Output

  • Write the value Q

Example

Input

5 8

1 6 4 2 9 

Output

6

Explanation: there are 5 sequence of consecutive days satisfying the constraint:

1

1 6

6

4

4 2
